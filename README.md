# BL Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

Live Dashboard: https://blretailsales.lovable.app/insights

## Project Overview

An interactive executive analytics web application built to evaluate unit economics, revenue distribution across customer demographics, and product category trends across 1,000 retail transactions.

---

## Executive Summary & Key Insights

* **Total Performance**: Generated **$460K** total revenue across **1,000 transactions** (2,512 total units sold) with an overall Average Order Value (AOV) of **$460**.
* **Core Revenue Demographic**: The **25–54 age group** drives **~$319K (~70%)** of total sales. The **35–44 cohort** leads with **$110.1K**, closely followed by the **25–34 group** at **$109.3K**.
* **Gender Split**: Spending is split evenly overall—**50.07% Female ($230K)** vs. **49.93% Male ($230K)**. However, women aged 25–44 form the largest single high-density revenue segment across Clothing and Beauty.
* **Category Breakdown**:
  * **Clothing**: Drives highest total revenue share at **34.7% ($159.7K)** across 869 units ($456 AOV).
  * **Electronics**: Contributes **32.9% ($151.5K)** with highest unit volume (870 units sold, $443 AOV).
  * **Beauty**: Generates **32.3% ($148.8K)** and leads overall basket value with an AOV of **$483** across 773 units.
* **Seasonality**: Revenue exhibits distinct cyclical peaks in **May ($45K+)**, **October**, and **December**, with a seasonal dip in **September ($24K)**.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

* **Demographic Targeting**: Reallocate digital ad spend toward high-density **female shoppers aged 25–44** to maximize return on ad spend (ROAS).
* **Cross-Selling & Bundling**: Launch premium product bundles in **Beauty** to leverage high customer spend capacity ($483 AOV). Implement volume-based loyalty incentives for **Clothing** and **Electronics**.
* **Inventory & Campaign Alignment**: Investigate operational factors (e.g., stockouts or delayed marketing) driving the September revenue trough to smooth out annual cash flow.

---

## Dashboard Structure

1. **Sales Overview Tab**: Executive KPI summary metrics, monthly cyclical trends, macro gender distribution, and category revenue share.
2. **Customer & Product Insights Tab**: Demographic breakdown (age cohort matrix), order values by product line, and detailed cross-tabulation tables.
